## Key Takeaways

* **36 is a Band, Not a Volume:** Saying "size 36" only specifies the ribcage band circumference, not breast volume. A 36A and a 36F have vastly different cup volumes.
* **34C > 34B:** Holding the band constant at 34, a **34C cup is 1 cup volume larger than a 34B**.
* **Specialty Sizing Rules:** Maternity bras require buying to fit on the tightest hook (to allow ribcage expansion), whereas everyday bras should fit on the loosest hook.

## Debunking Common Sizing Myths

### Myth 1: "Is 36 a breast size?"
Many people refer to "36" or "34" as a breast size. However, the number indicates your **underbust band size** in inches. Your true bra size requires both the band number and a cup letter (e.g. 36B, 36D, 36G).

### Myth 2: "Which is bigger, 34B or 34C?"
On the exact same 34-inch band:
* **34B:** Designed for a 36-inch full bust (2" difference).
* **34C:** Designed for a 37-inch full bust (3" difference).
* Therefore, **34C is larger in cup volume than 34B**.

If you need a roomier cup while keeping the same band snugness, move from 34B up to 34C. To keep the same cup volume on a looser band, use our [Sister Sizes Calculator](/sister-size-calculator).

## 2. Sports Bra Sizing: High-Impact vs Low-Impact

When choosing a sports bra in India, you will encounter two main sizing methods:

1. **Alpha Sizing (S, M, L, XL):** Common in low-to-medium impact bralettes (yoga, walking).
2. **Encapsulated Cup Sizing (e.g. 34D, 36DD):** Recommended for high-impact activities (running, HIIT, dancing) to support each breast individually.

```
Alpha Sizing Conversion Reference:
Small  (S)  --> 30C, 30D, 32A, 32B
Medium (M)  --> 32C, 32D, 34A, 34B
Large  (L)  --> 34C, 34D, 36A, 36B
X-Large (XL)--> 36C, 36D, 38B, 38C
```

For high-impact support, encapsulated cup-sized sports bras provide superior bounce control over compression-only bras. Explore our complete [Sports Bra Fitting Guide](/blog/sports-bra-size-calculator-guide).

## 3. Large Cup Sizes Explained: F, G, H, up to P Cups

Many shoppers ask **is there an F bra size**, or even a **P bra size**?

* **F & G Cups:** Very common in modern fuller-bust sizing. An F cup represents a 6-inch difference between overbust and underbust (in UK/Indian sizing), while a G cup represents a 7-inch difference.
* **H to P Cups:** Inclusive lingerie manufacturers (such as Elomi, Goddess, and Bravissimo) engineer supportive bras up to O and P cups for fuller-figured women.

```
Extended Cup Progression Scale:
D -> DD -> E -> F -> FF -> G -> GG -> H -> HH -> J -> K -> L -> M -> N -> O -> P
```

If you wear a large cup size, ensuring a firm band fit is essential—80% of bust support comes from the underbust band, not the shoulder straps.

## Where to Find Your Bra Size on a Bra Tag

If you are unsure where to check your current size:
1. Look at the **fabric tag sewn into the back band** near the hook-and-eye closure.
2. Check for printed brand labels inside the left cup wing.
3. Tags usually list multiple country columns (e.g. `US: 34D | UK: 34D | EU: 75D | FR: 90D`).
