## The Medical Mandate for Proper Support

The National Health Service (NHS) recognizes that a poorly fitting bra is a catalyst for a cascade of physical ailments. From chronic thoracic back pain and neck strain to severe dermatological issues caused by friction, the consequences of an inaccurate fit are profound. ### The Problem with Antiquated Sizing
Historically, many sizing guides recommended adding four or five inches to the underbust measurement to determine band size. NHS-aligned standards vehemently reject this approach. The underband must sit firm and level around the ribcage to bear 80% of the bust's weight. If the band is too loose, the burden shifts entirely to the shoulder straps, leading to deeply grooved shoulders, nerve compression, and tension headaches.

### Optimal Tissue Encapsulation
Furthermore, the cup must flawlessly encapsulate the breast tissue without spillage or compression. Medical standards dictate that the underwire must trace the natural crease of the breast root, resting perfectly flat against the sternum. Wire that sits on top of breast tissue can impede lymphatic drainage and cause localized trauma to delicate glandular structures.
