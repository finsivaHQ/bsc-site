## Key Takeaways

* **Unified Inch Band System:** Indian bra sizing primarily utilizes inch-based underbust band numbers (30, 32, 34, 36, 38, 40) aligned with North American and British standards.
* **Dual Tag Confusion:** Labels marking **32/80**, **34/85**, or **36/90** combine Indian/US inch band numbers with French (FR) centimeter underbust standards.
* **Metric Equivalents:** An Indian 34 band equals **75 cm in EU/Japan/Hong Kong/Korea**, **90 cm in France**, and **AU 12 in Australia**.

## Decoding Dual-Label Bra Tags in India (e.g. 32/80, 34/85, 36/90)

A frequent source of confusion when reading bra tags in Indian retail stores is seeing numbers separated by a slash, such as **32/80** or **34/85**.

<div class="bg-surface border border-hairline rounded-2xl p-6 my-6 shadow-xs">
  <h3 class="text-lg font-bold text-ink mb-2">📌 Decoding 32/80 & 34/85 Tags</h3>
  <p class="text-sm text-body leading-relaxed mb-3">
    These dual labels do not indicate two different size choices. Instead, they print the <strong>inch band size</strong> alongside the <strong>French centimeter band size</strong>:
  </p>
  <ul class="list-disc pl-5 text-sm text-body space-y-1">
    <li><strong>32/80:</strong> 32 inches underbust (IN/US/UK) = 80 cm French underbust (70 cm EU).</li>
    <li><strong>34/85:</strong> 34 inches underbust (IN/US/UK) = 85 cm French underbust (75 cm EU).</li>
    <li><strong>36/90:</strong> 36 inches underbust (IN/US/UK) = 90 cm French underbust (80 cm EU).</li>
  </ul>
</div>

Understanding this dual labeling prevents shoppers from mistakenly assuming "80" refers to a bust measurement in centimeters or a plus-size band.

## Cup Size Comparisons Across Regions

While band conversions follow static mathematical offsets, cup progressions diverge between North America and other regions starting at the 5th cup step (DD/E cups).

```
Bust-to-Underbust Difference Scale (Inches):
1" diff  --> A Cup (Universal)
2" diff  --> B Cup (Universal)
3" diff  --> C Cup (Universal)
4" diff  --> D Cup (Universal)
5" diff  --> DD (US/UK/AU/IN) | E Cup (EU/FR/JP/HK/KR)
6" diff  --> DDD/F (US)       | E Cup (UK) | F Cup (EU/IN)
```

When using an **indian bra size calculator uk**, **indian bra size calculator usa**, or **indian bra size calculator canada**, note that:
1. **India & UK:** Use single-letter progressions after D (D -> DD -> E -> F -> FF -> G).
2. **United States & Canada:** Use double-letter notation (D -> DD/E -> DDD/F -> G -> H).
3. **Europe, Japan & Korea:** Use sequential single letters without double letters (A -> B -> C -> D -> E -> F -> G -> H).

## Converting Indian Bra Sizes for Major International Brands

When ordering lingerie from international retailers to India or shopping abroad, use these regional guidelines:

### 1. Bra Size Chart India to Australia (AU/NZ)
Australian bra band sizes correspond directly to women's apparel dress sizing (AU 8, 10, 12, 14, 16). 
* **30 Band India** = AU 8
* **32 Band India** = AU 10
* **34 Band India** = AU 12
* **36 Band India** = AU 14

### 2. Bra Size Chart India to Europe (EU/FR)
European brands (like Triumph, Hunkemöller, or Chantelle) label bands in centimeters based on net underbust measurement minus 5–10 cm buffer.
* **32 Band India** = 70 EU / 85 FR
* **34 Band India** = 75 EU / 90 FR
* **36 Band India** = 80 EU / 95 FR

### 3. Bra Size Chart Us to India
US brands (such as Victoria's Secret, SKIMS, or Savage X Fenty) share identical inch band numbers with Indian sizes. However, for cup sizes above D (e.g., US DDD or G cups), convert to the equivalent Indian/UK cup letter using our [Bra Size Converter](/bra-size-converter).
