## Why Are Bra Sizes Different Across Countries?

The fundamental reason bra sizes differ globally stems from **historical apparel standards, unit systems (inches vs. centimeters), and cup progression rules**.

```
+-------------------+--------------------+------------------------+--------------------------+
| Country / Region  | Band Measurement   | Cup Increment System   | Example Size             |
+-------------------+--------------------+------------------------+--------------------------+
| United Kingdom    | Inches (Even)      | 1" per cup (UK Scale)   | 34FF                     |
| United States     | Inches (Even)      | 1" per cup (US Scale)   | 34H                      |
| European Union    | Centimeters (+15)  | 2 cm per cup           | 75H                      |
| Australia / NZ    | Dress Size Number  | 1" per cup (UK Scale)   | 12FF                     |
| Japan / East Asia | Centimeters        | 2.5 cm per cup         | G75                      |
+-------------------+--------------------+------------------------+--------------------------+
```

### 1. Imperial vs Metric Measurements (Are Bra Sizes in Inches or Cm?)
- **US, UK, Australia, New Zealand, & Canada**: Traditionally calculate band sizes using **inches**. However, how those inches map to cup progression varies.
- **European Union, France, Italy, & Japan**: Calculate band sizes and cup sizes strictly using **centimeters**. For instance, an EU band size 75 corresponds to an underbust measurement of approximately 73–77 cm.

### 2. Standardized vs Brand Variance (Are Bra Sizes Standardized?)
While national bodies (such as ISO standards in Europe or BS standards in the UK) outline baseline sizing, individual manufacturers introduce brand-specific variations. Brands like Victoria's Secret, Marks & Spencer, and Chantelle modify wire widths and cup depths, which is why your calculated size remains the baseline starting point.

## US vs UK Bra Sizes: How Do They Differ?

A major source of confusion occurs when comparing **US and UK bra sizing**. For A through D cups, US and UK sizes are identical. However, above a D cup, the two systems diverge dramatically.

```
Cup Difference (Inches) | UK Cup Letter | US Cup Letter
------------------------|---------------|--------------
1 inch                  | A             | A
2 inches                | B             | B
3 inches                | C             | C
4 inches                | D             | D
5 inches                | DD            | DD / E
6 inches                | E             | DDD / F
7 inches                | F             | G
8 inches                | FF            | H
9 inches                | G             | I
10 inches               | GG            | J
```

### Are Bra Sizes the Same in UK and US?
No! While a **34D** is identical in both systems, a UK **34FF** requires a US **34H**. UK sizing uses double letters (`DD`, `FF`, `GG`, `HH`) to indicate half-step depth increments, whereas US sizing typically uses single sequential letters (`D`, `DD/E`, `DDD/F`, `G`, `H`, `I`, `J`).

If you are buying bras from UK heritage brands (such as Panache, Freya, or Elomi), you **must** use your UK cup size. If you are buying from US brands (such as Wacoal or ThirdLove), use your US cup size.

## European Bra Sizing (EU, France, Italy): How EU Sizing Works

European bra sizing differs fundamentally from US/UK systems in two key ways:

1. **Band Math (+15 cm Rule)**: An EU band size is calculated by taking your actual underbust measurement in centimeters and rounding to the nearest 5 cm. For example, an underbust measurement of 75 cm maps to an EU band size of **75**. However, when converted to UK/US sizing, EU 75 equals a **34 band** (34 inches = 86.3 cm). The 11-15 cm difference accounts for built-in elastic stretch.
2. **Cup Increments**: EU cups progress in uniform 2 cm increments (`A`, `B`, `C`, `D`, `E`, `F`, `G`, `H`).

### Common EU to UK Conversions:
- **What bra size is 75B in UK?** An EU **75B** is equivalent to a **UK 34B** (US 34B).
- **What bra size is 85D in UK?** An EU **85D** is equivalent to a **UK 38D** (US 38D).
- **What bra size is 70G in UK?** An EU **70G** converts to a **UK 32F** (US 32G).

## Australian, New Zealand, & Canadian Bra Sizing

### How Do Bra Sizes Work in Australia & New Zealand (AU / NZ)?
Australia and New Zealand use **clothing dress sizes** for band labels instead of chest measurements in inches or centimeters:

```
US / UK Band Size | AU / NZ Band Size | Underbust (Inches)
------------------|-------------------|-------------------
30                | Size 8            | 28 - 30"
32                | Size 10           | 30 - 32"
34                | Size 12           | 32 - 34"
36                | Size 14           | 34 - 36"
38                | Size 16           | 36 - 38"
```
The cup progression in Australia follows the **UK cup lettering scale** (`A, B, C, D, DD, E, F, FF, G, GG`).

### How Do Bra Sizes Work in Canada?
Canadian sizing mirrors **US bra sizing** almost completely. Bands are measured in inches (even numbers 30, 32, 34, 36, etc.) and cups follow the US scale (`A, B, C, D, DD, DDD/E, G, H`).

## Japanese Bra Sizing: Are Bra Sizes in Japan Different?

Yes, Japanese bra sizing is unique:
- **Band Numbers**: Expressed in centimeters (`65, 70, 75, 80, 85`).
- **Cup Letters**: Lettering begins with `A` and proceeds `B, C, D, E, F, G, H`. However, Japanese cups are cut noticeably shallower and narrower than Western cups.
- **Fit Adjustment Rule**: Western shoppers buying Japanese lingerie (such as Wacoal Japan, Peach John, or Salute) typically need to **go up 1 to 2 cup sizes** (e.g., a US 32D usually fits a Japanese 70E or 70F).

## Essential Rules of International Bra Sizing

1. **Are Bra Sizes Only Even Numbers?**
   Yes, in US, UK, EU, and AU standard charts, band sizes are designated by even numbers (30, 32, 34, 36, 38, etc.). If your body underbust measures 31 inches, you should test both 32 bands and 30 bands with a sister size adjustment. Read our full guide on [Sister Sizes Explained](/blog/sister-sizes-explained).
2. **Always Check the Country of Origin**: When buying online, check whether the retailer uses their native country sizing or local converted sizing.
3. **Use Accurate Body Measurements**: Never rely on tag sizes from old bras. Measure your underbust and bust in both inches and centimeters before buying. Refer to our [Step-by-Step Measuring Guide](/how-to-measure).
