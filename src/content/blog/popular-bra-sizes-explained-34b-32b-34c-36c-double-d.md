If you look at lingerie sales statistics across North America and Europe, five bra sizes dominate retail displays: **32B**, **34B**, **34C**, **36C**, and **Double D (DD)**. 

Because these five sizes are produced in massive quantities, millions of women end up buying them by default. However, fitting specialists estimate that **over 70% of women wearing a 34B or 36C are actually wearing the wrong size**—often needing a smaller band (like 30 or 32) paired with a larger cup (like D or DD).

## Detailed Size Profiles: Which One Are You?

### 1. The 32B Bra Size: Petite Ribcage with Compact Projection
- **Underbust**: 31.5 to 32.5 inches
- **Overbust**: 33.5 to 34.5 inches (2-inch difference = B cup)
- **Volume**: ~240cc per breast
- **Fit Profile**: Common among slender, athletic, or petite body frames. 
- **Sister Sizes**: **30C** (smaller band, same cup volume) and **34A** (larger band, same cup volume).

### 2. The 34B Bra Size: Lingerie's Historical "Default"
- **Underbust**: 33.5 to 34.5 inches
- **Overbust**: 35.5 to 36.5 inches (2-inch difference = B cup)
- **Volume**: ~390cc per breast
- **Fit Profile**: Often called the "universal starter size." However, many women who buy 34B actually measure as a **30D** or **32C** when using proper tape pressure!
- **Sister Sizes**: **32C** (smaller band) and **36A** (larger band).

### 3. The 34C Bra Size: Balanced Proportional Medium
- **Underbust**: 33.5 to 34.5 inches
- **Overbust**: 36.5 to 37.5 inches (3-inch difference = C cup)
- **Volume**: ~480cc per breast
- **Fit Profile**: A balanced silhouette with moderate forward projection.
- **Sister Sizes**: **32D** (smaller band) and **36B** (larger band).

### 4. The 36C Bra Size: Full Medium on a Broader Frame
- **Underbust**: 35.5 to 36.5 inches
- **Overbust**: 38.5 to 39.5 inches (3-inch difference = C cup)
- **Volume**: ~590cc per breast
- **Fit Profile**: Offers fuller volume across a broader ribcage.
- **Sister Sizes**: **34D** (smaller band) and **38B** (larger band).

### 5. The Double D (34DD) Bra Size: High Volume & Projection
- **Underbust**: 33.5 to 34.5 inches
- **Overbust**: 38.5 to 39.5 inches (5-inch difference = DD cup)
- **Volume**: ~710cc per breast
- **Fit Profile**: High projection requiring structured underwire support.
- **Sister Sizes**: **32DDD / 32E** (smaller band) and **36D** (larger band).

## Sister Sizes Chart for 32B, 34B, 34C, 36C, and DD

Sister sizing allows you to maintain the **exact same cup volume** while adjusting the band for tighter or looser ribcage comfort:

```

----------------------|---------------------------|---------------|---------------------------
~240 cc Volume        | 30C                       | 32B           | 34A
```

## Why You Might Be Wearing the Wrong Size (The 34B / 36C Myth)

Standard department stores stock mostly 34B, 34C, 36B, and 36C bras because manufacturing a narrow range of sizes reduces inventory costs. As a result:

1. **Women with a 30-inch ribcage** are often told to buy a **34B**, causing the band to ride up and straps to slip.
2. **Women with a 32-inch ribcage** who need a **32DD** are sold a **36C**, causing breast tissue to spill out the sides or underwires to dig in.

### Quick Self-Test:
- Put on your bra and stand in front of a mirror.
- Raise both arms above your head.
- **Does your bra band ride up your back or lift off your breasts?** 
  - If YES, your band is too large! Drop down 1 or 2 band sizes and increase your cup letter.

2. **Overbust (Bust)**: Measure around the fullest part of your breasts. Keep tape level.

## Expert Advisory & Medical Perspective

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Medical Advisory on Support & Posture**:
> A band that is too large (such as wearing a 36C when you measure 32E) shifts 100% of the breast weight onto your shoulder straps. This puts chronic strain on the trapezius and levator scapulae muscles, frequently leading to tension headaches, neck stiffness, and bra strap grooves.
