## What Makes Full-Figure & Plus-Size Bra Sizing Unique?

In standard bra design, scaling up a size simply means adding material. However, **full-figure and plus-size engineering** requires structural reinforcement:

- **Soft Tissue Mobility:** Plus-size breast tissue is often softer and more malleable, requiring deeper cup molding or multi-part seamed construction to lift and center.
- **Back Band Surface Area:** Narrow 2-hook bands dig into side tissue. High-quality plus-size bras feature **3 to 5 hook-and-eye closures** with wide leotard backs to smooth ribcage contours.
- **Ergonomic Shoulder Straps:** Padded, center-set shoulder straps prevent slipping and prevent shoulder groove indentation.

> **Fit Rule:** If your bra band rolls up under your bust or rides up toward your shoulder blades, your band is either too small or the elastic lacks structural side boning.

## How to Measure for Plus Size Bras Accurately

Measuring at home ensures you order the correct size online during **plus size bra sales in Canada**:

1. **Underbust Band:** Wrap the tape snugly around your ribcage directly under your breasts. Exhale fully. Note the inch size (e.g., 40 inches).
2. **Fullest Bust:** Wrap the tape loosely around the peak of your chest. If breasts are pendulous, lean forward slightly at a 45-degree angle to capture full tissue depth.
3. **Calculate Difference:** Subtract underbust from bust.
   - 4 in diff = D | 5 in diff = DD | 6 in diff = DDD | 7 in diff = G | 8 in diff = H
4. Input your figures into our [Canada Bra Size Calculator](/#calculator).
