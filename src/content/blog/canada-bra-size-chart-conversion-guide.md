## The Official Canadian Bra Sizing Standard

Canada shares its primary bra sizing standard with the United States (US/CA standard). 

- **Band Sizes:** Expressed in even numbers representing inches (28, 30, 32, 34, 36, 38, 40, 42, 44, 46, 48).
- **Cup Sizes:** Expressed in letters based on the inch difference between underbust and bust measurements:
  `AA -> A -> B -> C -> D -> DD (E) -> DDD (F) -> G -> H -> I -> J -> K`

When you consult a **bra measurement chart in Canada**, the band size reflects your ribcage size, while the cup letter reflects breast volume relative to that specific band size.

## What is 75B & 80B Bra Size in Canada?

Two of the most frequently searched European conversion queries in Canada are:

### 1. 75B Bra Size in Canada
- **European Label:** 75B
- **Canadian Equivalent:** **34B**
- **Underbust Measurement:** ~73–77 cm (29–30 inches)
- **Bust Measurement:** ~87–89 cm (34–35 inches)
- **Fit Notes:** If a European 75B bra feels snug in the band, its closest Canadian sister size is **36A** (or **32C** for a firmer band).

### 2. 80B Bra Size in Canada
- **European Label:** 80B
- **Canadian Equivalent:** **36B**
- **Underbust Measurement:** ~78–82 cm (31–32 inches)
- **Bust Measurement:** ~92–94 cm (36–37 inches)
- **Fit Notes:** The Canadian equivalent 36B provides identical cup volume to a 34C or 38A.

## UK vs Canadian Bra Size Conversion

If you purchase popular British lingerie brands in Canada (like Panache, Freya, Elomi, or Fantasie), take note of cup divergence:

```
US/CA Cup:  D -> DD -> DDD -> G  -> H  -> I  -> J
UK Cup:     D -> DD -> E   -> F  -> FF -> G  -> GG
```

- Up to **DD cup**, Canadian and UK sizes are identical.
- For a Canadian **DDD cup**, order a UK **E cup**.
- For a Canadian **G cup**, order a UK **F cup**.
- For a Canadian **H cup**, order a UK **FF cup**.

## Australia (AU/NZ) & French (FR) Conversions

Canadian shoppers traveling or buying overseas brands often encounter Australian or French labeling:

- **Australian Sizing (AU/NZ):** Uses band numbers derived from dress sizes (Size 8 = 30 band, Size 10 = 32 band, Size 12 = 34 band, Size 14 = 36 band, Size 16 = 38 band).
- **French Sizing (FR):** Adds **15 cm** to the EU band size (e.g., an EU 75 band becomes a French 90 band, which equals a Canadian 34 band).
