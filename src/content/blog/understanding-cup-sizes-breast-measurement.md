## The Mystery of Cup Sizes

If you have ever typed **"what cup size am I calculator"** into a search engine, you are among millions of women trying to decode the lingerie industry's sizing systems. Cup sizes are notoriously misunderstood. Many people believe an "A" cup is universally small, and a "D" cup is universally massive. In reality, cup sizes are completely relative to your band size.

In this article, we'll explain **how does cup size measure**, what the letters actually mean, and how you can use a **find bra size calculator** to ensure you are wearing the correct size.

### What is ABCD Bra Size?

The letters in your bra size (A, B, C, D) represent the volume of your breast tissue relative to your ribcage. Specifically, they represent the difference in inches between your overbust and your underbust. 

*   **A Cup:** 1-inch difference
*   **B Cup:** 2-inch difference
*   **C Cup:** 3-inch difference
*   **D Cup:** 4-inch difference

If you are asking, **what's my cup size calculator**, you simply need to subtract your underbust measurement from your overbust measurement.

### How Is Bra Cup Size Calculator Math Done?

Let's break down **how is bra cup size calculator** math performed in the background of our tools. 

If your underbust measures 32 inches, and your overbust measures 37 inches, the difference is 5 inches. Looking at the standard chart:
*   1" = A
*   2" = B
*   3" = C
*   4" = D
*   5" = DD (or E in UK sizing)

Therefore, your size would be a 32DD. If you don't want to do the math manually, you can instantly find your result using our **[What Size Bra Should I Wear Calculator](/#calculator)**.

### Is 30E A Big Bra Size?

A common question we receive is, **"is 30E a big bra size?"** 

Because of outdated fitting methods, many people assume anything above a D cup is enormous. In reality, a 30E simply means a 30-inch underbust and a 36-inch overbust (a 6-inch difference). This is a very average, proportionate size. Someone who is a 30E is often incorrectly fitted into a 34C because many stores don't stock bands below a 32. 

### What Is Maximum Bra Size?

You might also be wondering, **what is maximum bra size?** While many department stores stop at a DDD or G cup, specialized full-bust brands (like Panache, Freya, and Elomi) produce bras up to UK K, L, and even M cups (which corresponds to a 13 to 15-inch difference!). There is no true "maximum," as everyone's body is unique.
