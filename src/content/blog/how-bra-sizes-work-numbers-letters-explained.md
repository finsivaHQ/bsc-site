## The Core Formula: How Bra Size Is Calculated

A bra size consists of two parts: a **Number** (Band Size) and a **Letter** (Cup Size).

```
   [ Band Size (Number) ]   +   [ Cup Size (Letter) ]   =   [ Full Bra Size ]
   Underbust Measurement       (Bust - Underbust) Math         e.g., 34D
```

### 1. How Bra Size Numbers Work (The Band Size)
Your band size represents the circumference of your ribcage directly under your breasts. 
- **Underbust Measurement**: Snugly measure around your ribcage.
- **Band Calculation**: If your underbust measurement is an even number (e.g., 34 inches), that is generally your band size. If it is an odd number (e.g., 33 inches), standard sizing rules specify rounding up or down depending on muscle tone, rib structure, and brand stretch.

### 2. How Bra Size Letters Work (The Cup Volume)
Cup letters do **not** represent static volume; they represent the relative difference between your overbust (fullest part of breasts) and underbust measurements.

$$\text{Cup Difference (Inches)} = \text{Bust Measurement} - \text{Underbust Measurement}$$

```
Difference (Inches) | US / Standard Cup | UK Cup Letter | What It Means
--------------------|-------------------|---------------|-----------------------------
1 inch              | A                 | A             | 1" greater than underbust
2 inches            | B                 | B             | 2" greater than underbust
3 inches            | C                 | C             | 3" greater than underbust
4 inches            | D                 | D             | 4" greater than underbust
5 inches            | DD / E            | DD            | 5" greater than underbust
6 inches            | DDD / F           | E             | 6" greater than underbust
7 inches            | G                 | F             | 7" greater than underbust
8 inches            | H                 | FF            | 8" greater than underbust
```

## Where Do Bra Sizes Start & How Do They Progression?

### Where Do Bra Sizes Start?
Standard commercial bra sizes start at a **28A or 30AA** for small busts/teens and extend up to band sizes of 56+ and cup sizes up to US P / UK K cups and beyond.

### How Do Bra Sizes Go Up in Order?
When bra sizes go up, they can increase in two separate directions:

1. **Band Increase (e.g., 32D -> 34D -> 36D)**: The ribcage band gets larger, and because cup size is proportional, the cup volume *also* increases slightly even though the letter remains D!
2. **Cup Increase (e.g., 34A -> 34B -> 34C -> 34D -> 34DD)**: The ribcage band remains constant at 34 inches, while each letter step adds 1 inch of projection/depth to the cup.

## Demystifying Cup Volume: Why Cup Sizes Are Not Fixed

One of the biggest fitting myths is assuming a D cup is always "large." A D cup on a 30 band contains significantly less volume than a D cup on a 40 band!

```
Bra Size | Band Underbust | Bust Circumference | Approximate Cup Volume
---------|----------------|--------------------|------------------------
30D      | 30 inches      | 34 inches          | ~390 cc
34D      | 34 inches      | 38 inches          | ~590 cc
38D      | 38 inches      | 42 inches          | ~850 cc
```

### Sister Sizes: Equivalent Cup Volumes Explained
Because cup volume scales with band size, different bra sizes can hold the **exact same cup volume**. These are known as **Sister Sizes**.

- **Which bra size is equivalent to 34D?** 
  - **32DD** (Smaller band, larger cup letter = same volume)
  - **36C** (Larger band, smaller cup letter = same volume)

- **Which bra size is equivalent to 36DD?**
  - **34DDD / 34F** (Smaller band, larger letter)
  - **38D** (Larger band, smaller letter)

- **Which bra size is equivalent to 38DD?**
  - **36DDD / 36F**
  - **40D**

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Sister Size Rule**: If your band feels too loose but the cups fit nicely, **go down one band size and up one cup letter** (e.g., move from 36C to 34D). If your band feels painfully tight, **go up one band size and down one cup letter** (e.g., move from 34D to 36C). Try our [Sister Size Calculator](/sister-size-calculator) for instant mapping.

## Why Is Bra Size ABCD & Why Double Letters (DD)?

Historical lingerie standards initially categorized busts into simple A, B, C, D categories in the 1930s. As women's body diversity became better understood and textile manufacturing modernized, manufacturers needed finer increments.

- **Why Bra Size DD?** "DD" was added as an intermediate step between D and E to accommodate a 5-inch bust-to-underbust differential without redesigning wire molds.
- **UK Sizing Progression**: UK manufacturers introduced double letters (`DD`, `FF`, `GG`, `HH`) to maintain consistent 1-inch cup depth increments throughout large bust ranges.

## Medical & Ergonomic Perspectives

> [!NOTE]
> **Doctor & Fitting Specialist Advice**:
> Wearing a bra where the band is too large and the cup is too small shifts 80% of the breast weight onto the shoulder straps, leading to chronic cervical spine tension, trapezius strain, and skin grooving. A properly calculated band supports 80–90% of the bust weight from the ribcage, releasing shoulder stress.
