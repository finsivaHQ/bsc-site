## What Is Income Tax in Pakistan?

**Income tax in Pakistan** is a direct federal tax levied on individual earnings, corporate profits, and capital gains. It is governed by the **Income Tax Ordinance, 2001** and enforced by the **Federal Board of Revenue (FBR)**.

```
   Total Gross Income   -   Allowable Deductions   =   Taxable Income
           |
           v
   Apply FBR Tax Slab   =   Annual Tax Liability
```

### Who Has to Pay Income Tax in Pakistan?
Under FBR regulations, you are required to file a tax return and pay tax if:
1. You are a **resident individual** whose annual taxable income exceeds **PKR 600,000**.
2. You own business assets, real estate, or commercial vehicles meeting specified FBR filing thresholds.
3. You are a corporate entity, partnership (AOP), or company operating in Pakistan.

## Is Pakistan Tax Free? (Tax-Free Thresholds & Exemptions)

Many international observers ask: *"Is Pakistan tax free?"* The simple answer is **no**. However, Pakistan provides a generous basic tax-free allowance for lower-income earners.

- **Basic Exemption Limit**: Anyone earning **up to PKR 600,000 per year** (PKR 50,000 per month) pays **0% income tax**.
- **Overseas Remittances**: Remittances sent by overseas Pakistanis through official banking channels are exempt from income tax. Read our detailed guide on [Overseas Pakistani Tax Exemptions](/blog/overseas-pakistani-tax-exemption-guide).

## Official FBR Income Tax Slabs & Rates

FBR categorizes individual taxpayers into two primary groups:
1. **Salaried Individuals**: Where salary income accounts for **75% or more** of total taxable income.
2. **Non-Salaried Individuals & Business Owners**: Where salary is less than 75% of total income.

### 1. Salaried Individuals Tax Slabs

```
Annual Taxable Income (PKR)  | Tax Rate / Tax Calculation Formula
-----------------------------|----------------------------------------------------
Up to Rs. 600,000            | 0% (Tax Free)
Rs. 600,001 to 1,200,000     | 5% of the amount exceeding Rs. 600,000
Rs. 1,200,001 to 2,200,000   | Rs. 30,000 + 15% of the amount exceeding Rs. 1.2M
Rs. 2,200,001 to 3,200,000   | Rs. 180,000 + 25% of the amount exceeding Rs. 2.2M
Rs. 3,200,001 to 4,100,000   | Rs. 430,000 + 30% of the amount exceeding Rs. 3.2M
Above Rs. 4,100,000          | Rs. 700,000 + 35% of the amount exceeding Rs. 4.1M
```

### 2. Non-Salaried & Business Tax Slabs

```
Annual Taxable Income (PKR)  | Tax Rate / Tax Calculation Formula
-----------------------------|----------------------------------------------------
Up to Rs. 600,000            | 0% (Tax Free)
Rs. 600,001 to 1,200,000     | 15% of the amount exceeding Rs. 600,000
Rs. 1,200,001 to 1,600,000   | Rs. 90,000 + 20% of the amount exceeding Rs. 1.2M
Rs. 1,600,001 to 3,200,000   | Rs. 170,000 + 30% of the amount exceeding Rs. 1.6M
Rs. 3,200,001 to 5,600,000   | Rs. 650,000 + 40% of the amount exceeding Rs. 3.2M
Above Rs. 5,600,000          | Rs. 1,610,000 + 45% of the amount exceeding Rs. 5.6M
```

## How Much Income Tax in Pakistan? Real Examples

### Example A: Monthly Salary of PKR 150,000
- **Annual Gross Salary** = $150,000 \times 12 = \text{PKR } 1,800,000$
- **Tax Bracket**: Fits slab 3 (PKR 1.2M to 2.2M)
- **Base Tax**: PKR 30,000
- **Variable Tax**: $15\% \text{ of } (1,800,000 - 1,200,000) = 0.15 \times 600,000 = \text{PKR } 90,000$
- **Total Annual Tax** = $30,000 + 90,000 = \text{PKR } 120,000$
- **Monthly Tax Deduction** = $\text{PKR } 10,000$ per month.
- **Monthly Take-Home Pay** = $\text{PKR } 140,000$.

### Example B: Monthly Salary of PKR 300,000
- **Annual Gross Salary** = $\text{PKR } 3,600,000$
- **Tax Bracket**: Fits slab 5 (PKR 3.2M to 4.1M)
- **Total Annual Tax** = $430,000 + 30\% \times (3,600,000 - 3,200,000) = 430,000 + 120,000 = \text{PKR } 550,000$
- **Monthly Tax Deduction** = $\text{PKR } 45,833$.

> [!NOTE]
> **Filer vs Non-Filer Status**:
> Registering on the FBR Active Taxpayers List (ATL) gives you "Filer" status, significantly reducing withholding taxes on banking transactions, property buying, and vehicle purchases.
