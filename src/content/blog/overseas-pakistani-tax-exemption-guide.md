## 1. Section 82 Rules: Are Overseas Pakistanis Exempt From Income Tax?

Under **Section 82 of the Income Tax Ordinance, 2001**, tax liability in Pakistan is determined by **residency status**, not citizenship.

```
Stay in Pakistan during Tax Year (July 1 - June 30):
   |
   +---> 183 days or more  ======> RESIDENT (World Income Taxable)
   |
   +---> Less than 183 days =====> NON-RESIDENT (Foreign Income EXEMPT!)
```

### Key Residency Rules:
1. **Non-Resident Status**: If you stay in Pakistan for **less than 183 days** during a fiscal year (July 1 to June 30), you are classified as a **non-resident**.
2. **Tax Immunity on Foreign Earnings**: As a non-resident, your salary, business profits, and investment income earned outside Pakistan are **100% exempt from Pakistan income tax**.
3. **Pakistan-Sourced Income**: If a non-resident owns real estate, businesses, or stocks inside Pakistan, the income generated from those local assets is subject to standard FBR withholding tax rates.

## 2. Tax Exemption on Foreign Remittances (Section 111)

To encourage foreign exchange inflows, the Government of Pakistan provides explicit statutory tax protection for foreign bank transfers.

> [!NOTE]
> **Section 111(4) Protection**:
> Money remitted from abroad into Pakistan through official banking channels (SWIFT, banking transfers, wire transfers) cannot be questioned by FBR regarding its origin, and no income tax can be levied on such remittances.

### Roshan Digital Account (RDA) Benefits:
Overseas Pakistanis investing through **Roshan Digital Accounts (RDA)** enjoy:
- Full exemption from filing tax returns solely due to RDA holdings.
- Final tax rate (15-30%) automatically withheld on profit from Naya Pakistan Certificates (NPC) and stock market gains, with no further tax liability.

## Expert Advisory for Non-Resident Filers

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Filer Status for Overseas Pakistanis**:
> Even though foreign income is exempt, many overseas Pakistanis choose to file a zero-tax return in Pakistan to maintain **"Filer" status**. Being on the Active Taxpayers List (ATL) prevents high non-filer withholding taxes when buying property, opening bank accounts, or purchasing motor vehicles in Pakistan.

Visit the [FBR Iris Official Portal](https://iris.fbr.gov.pk/) to submit non-resident tax declarations online.
