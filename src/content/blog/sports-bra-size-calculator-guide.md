## Frequently Asked Questions

<details class="bg-surface border border-hairline rounded-xl p-5 mt-4 cursor-pointer">
  <summary class="font-bold text-ink list-none flex justify-between">
    <span>Is a sports bra size the same as a normal bra size?</span>
  </summary>
  <p class="mt-4 text-body text-sm">Not always. Because sports bras require a firmer hold, you may find that you prefer a slightly tighter band or a different cup volume depending on whether the bra uses compression or encapsulation. Always measure specifically for sports wear.</p>
</details>

<details class="bg-surface border border-hairline rounded-xl p-5 mt-4 cursor-pointer">
  <summary class="font-bold text-ink list-none flex justify-between">
    <span>How long does a sports bra last?</span>
  </summary>
  <p class="mt-4 text-body text-sm">A sports bra should never celebrate its first birthday if you use it regularly. With average use (washing after every sweaty workout), the elastic degrades in about 6 to 12 months. When the band loses its stretch, it's time to replace it.</p>
</details>

<details class="bg-surface border border-hairline rounded-xl p-5 mt-4 cursor-pointer">
  <summary class="font-bold text-ink list-none flex justify-between">
    <span>Should my sports bra have underwire?</span>
  </summary>
  <p class="mt-4 text-body text-sm">This is personal preference. For D+ cups, underwires wrapped in silicone or heavy padding offer superior encapsulation and support for high-impact activities. However, many modern wire-free sports bras use advanced fabrics that provide equal support without the wire.</p>
</details>
