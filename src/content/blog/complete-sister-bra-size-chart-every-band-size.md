## The Philosophy of Sister Sizing

In the realm of premium lingerie, precision is paramount. Sister sizes are alternative bra sizes where the volume of the cup remains exactly the same, even though the band size and cup letter change. This ingenious system is rooted in the fact that cup size is not absolute; it is relative to the band. 

When you go up in band size, the cup volume increases if you stay with the same letter. Therefore, to maintain the same cup volume on a larger band, you must go down a cup letter. Conversely, if you desire a tighter, more supportive band, you would decrease the band size and increase the cup letter. It is a delicate balance, a refined adjustment that can transform a good bra into a spectacular one.

For example, if you find that a 34C fits perfectly in the cups but the band is uncomfortably snug, you might reach for a 36B. The volume holding the breast tissue is identical, but the band offers more breathing room.

## The Art of the Perfect Try-On

Armed with your sister size knowledge, the fitting room becomes a sanctuary of discovery rather than a chamber of frustration. Remember that every brand has its own unique sizing quirks, influenced by the stretch of the fabric, the shape of the underwire, and the cut of the cups. 

When you test a sister size, focus on the fundamental pillars of fit:
1.  **The Band:** It should sit parallel to the floor, firm but not constricting. It should not ride up your back.
2.  **The Gore:** The center piece between the cups should tack flat against your sternum (unless you are wearing a wireless style).
3.  **The Cups:** There should be no spillage over the top (quad-boob) and no gaping or wrinkling in the fabric. The underwire should encompass your entire breast tissue without resting on it.
