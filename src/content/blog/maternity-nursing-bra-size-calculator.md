<details class="bg-surface border border-hairline rounded-xl p-5 mt-4 cursor-pointer">
  <summary class="font-bold text-ink list-none flex justify-between">
    <span>How often should I re-calculate my size during pregnancy?</span>
  </summary>
  <p class="mt-4 text-body text-sm">We recommend measuring at the end of your first trimester, around week 20, around week 36 (for nursing bras), and then 6-8 weeks postpartum when your milk supply has regulated.</p>
</details>

<details class="bg-surface border border-hairline rounded-xl p-5 mt-4 cursor-pointer">
  <summary class="font-bold text-ink list-none flex justify-between">
    <span>Can I wear underwire bras while pregnant or nursing?</span>
  </summary>
  <p class="mt-4 text-body text-sm">It is generally advised to avoid rigid underwires as they can compress expanding breast tissue and milk ducts, leading to blocked ducts or mastitis. If you prefer wires, look for flexible, soft-wire options specifically designed for maternity.</p>
</details>

<details class="bg-surface border border-hairline rounded-xl p-5 mt-4 cursor-pointer">
  <summary class="font-bold text-ink list-none flex justify-between">
    <span>What if my breasts are two different sizes?</span>
  </summary>
  <p class="mt-4 text-body text-sm">This is very common, especially when breastfeeding! Always measure and buy a bra that fits the larger breast. You can tighten the strap on the smaller side or use a nursing pad to even out the fit.</p>
</details>

<details class="bg-surface border border-hairline rounded-xl p-5 mt-4 cursor-pointer">
  <summary class="font-bold text-ink list-none flex justify-between">
    <span>How many nursing bras do I actually need?</span>
  </summary>
  <p class="mt-4 text-body text-sm">A good rule of thumb is three: one to wear, one in the wash, and one in the drawer ready to go. You may also want a dedicated soft sleep bra for nighttime nursing.</p>
</details>
