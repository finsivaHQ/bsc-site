## The Science of Large Bust Support & Health Impact

Breasts are composed of glandular tissue, fat, and Cooper's ligaments. Breasts do not contain muscular tissue; therefore, gravitational pull must be supported externally by a correctly engineered bra band.

When a **bra for big bust in Canada** lacks sufficient band support:
- **Spinal Misalignment:** The weight of D+ breasts (which can weigh 2 to 6+ lbs) pulls the cervical spine forward, leading to chronic upper back tension.
- **Shoulder Grooving:** Narrow straps cut into the trapezius muscle, potentially causing nerve compression (thoracic outlet syndrome).
- **Skin Irritation:** Inadequate lift causes intertrigo (chafing and heat rash under the breast fold).

## How to Solve Common Large Bust Fit Issues

If you experience these common fit issues, follow these fixes:

1. **Underwire Digging into Ribs:** Your cup is too small or too shallow! The wire is being pushed downward. Increase your cup size by 1–2 letters (e.g., 36D to 36DD).
2. **Double-Boob / Quad Spillage:** Cup volume is too small. Try 1 to 2 cup sizes larger or use our [Sister Size Calculator](/sister-size-calculator).
3. **Bra Band Riding Up Back:** Band is too loose! Go down one band size (e.g., from 38DD to 36DDD).
4. **Straps Digging Into Shoulders:** Your band is not carrying the load. Tighten your band or size down in band width.
