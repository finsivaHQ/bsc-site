## Why Bra Sizing Varies Across Canadian Brands

Even though Canadian brands nominally follow US/CA sizing standards, fit variations occur due to:

- **Fit Model Variation:** Brands design patterns using different live fit models. A junior-leaning brand uses narrower wire gauges than a full-figure brand.
- **Fabric Stretch & Elasticity:** High-spandex lace bands stretch more over time than rigid satin or power-mesh bands.
- **Cup Mold Depth:** Heavy foam padding reduces inner cup volume by 0.5 to 1 full cup size.

```
                      BRAND CUP PROJECTION COMPARISON
  [La Senza Push-Up]       -> Shallow cup depth; padded base (Order +1 Cup)
  [Wonderbra T-Shirt Bra]  -> Moderate cup depth; rounded contour (True to Size)
  [Glamorise MagicLift]    -> Deep cup depth; multi-part seamed lift (True to Size)
```
