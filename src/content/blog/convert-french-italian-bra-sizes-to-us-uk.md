# Shopping European Lingerie Online: How to Convert French and Italian Bra Sizes to US/UK

The world of luxury European lingerie is alluring. Brands from Paris and Milan offer exquisite lace, impeccable craftsmanship, and unique silhouettes. However, for buyers in the US or UK, the thrill of finding a gorgeous Chantelle, Aubade, or La Perla bra online is often immediately ruined by the sizing drop-down menu. 

*What on earth is a size 90D? Or a size II?*

International bra sizing is notoriously convoluted because there is no globally enforced standard. If you want to shop European lingerie with confidence and avoid costly international returns, you must understand how French and Italian sizing systems map back to your US or UK size.

## The Foundation: Understanding EU Sizing

Before jumping into French and Italian specifics, you must understand the standard European (EU) sizing system, as it forms the baseline.

In the US and UK, band sizes are measured in inches (32, 34, 36). In the EU, band sizes are measured in centimeters (70, 75, 80). 
*   US/UK 32 = EU 70
*   US/UK 34 = EU 75
*   US/UK 36 = EU 80

EU cup sizes follow a strict alphabetical order without skipping or doubling letters (except occasionally skipping 'I'). They go A, B, C, D, E, F, G. 
This differs from the US (D, DD, DDD, G) and the UK (D, DD, E, F, FF).

## Decoding French (FR) Bra Sizing

French bra sizing is unique and, mathematically speaking, a bit strange. 

**The Band:** French band sizes are calculated by taking the EU band size and adding 15. This is purely historical and doesn't map directly to a physical measurement in inches or centimeters without doing the math.
*   EU 70 + 15 = **FR 85** (Equivalent to US/UK 32)
*   EU 75 + 15 = **FR 90** (Equivalent to US/UK 34)
*   EU 80 + 15 = **FR 95** (Equivalent to US/UK 36)

**The Cup:** French cup sizes generally follow the EU standard (A, B, C, D, E, F, G). If you are a US 34DD, your EU size is roughly a 75E. Therefore, your French size would be a **90E**. 

## Decoding Italian (IT) Bra Sizing

Italian bra sizing takes a completely different approach for band sizes, utilizing Roman numerals. 

**The Band:** 
*   IT I (1) = US/UK 32 = EU 70 = FR 85
*   IT II (2) = US/UK 34 = EU 75 = FR 90
*   IT III (3) = US/UK 36 = EU 80 = FR 95
*   IT IV (4) = US/UK 38 = EU 85 = FR 100

**The Cup:** Italian cup sizes usually follow the standard EU progression (A, B, C, D, E). So, if you are a US 36C, your Italian size is simply a **III C**. 

## The Tricky Territory: Cup Conversions

The most dangerous pitfall when shopping international lingerie is the cup size, particularly if you wear a DD or larger. 

Let's look at the progression after a D cup:
*   **US:** D, DD, DDD, G, H
*   **UK:** D, DD, E, F, FF
*   **EU/FR/IT:** D, E, F, G, H

If you wear a UK 32E, you might assume you wear an EU 70E. However, looking at the progression above, the 5th cup volume in the UK is 'DD', but the 5th in the EU is 'E'. A UK 'E' is actually the 6th volume, meaning your EU equivalent is actually a 70F. 

Always check a brand's specific sizing chart. Many European brands skip the 'DD' entirely and go straight from D to E.
