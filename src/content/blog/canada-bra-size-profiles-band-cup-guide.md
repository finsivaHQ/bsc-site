## Petite & Small Band Profiles (Bands 28, 30, 32)

Petite band sizes cater to narrow ribcages (underbust measurements of 27 to 32 inches).

```
                      BAND 28 - 32 PROFILE HIGHLIGHTS
  Underbust Baseline: 27 in - 32 in
  Common Cup Spectrum: AA, A, B, C, D, DD, DDD, E, F, FF, G, H
  Fit Focus: Snug band anchor prevents strap slipping on narrow shoulders
```

### 1. Band Size 28 Profiles
- **28D Bra Canada:** Underbust ~27–28 in | Bust ~31–32 in. Sister size: **30C**. Excellent for slim frames with moderate bust projection.
- **28DD Bra Canada:** Underbust ~27–28 in | Bust ~32–33 in. Sister size: **30D**. Full-bust petite profile requiring narrow wire gores.

### 2. Band Size 30 Profiles
- **30A Bra Canada:** Underbust ~29–30 in | Bust ~30–31 in. Sister size: **32AA**. Shallow profile; best in soft bralettes.
- **30B Bra Canada:** Underbust ~29–30 in | Bust ~31–32 in. Sister size: **32A**. Natural moderate curve; gap-free in demi cups.
- **30C Bra Canada:** Underbust ~29–30 in | Bust ~32–33 in. Sister size: **32B**. Balanced volume for narrow shoulders.
- **30D Bra Canada:** Underbust ~29–30 in | Bust ~33–34 in. Sister size: **32C**.
- **30DD Bra Canada / 30DDD Bra Canada:** Underbust ~29–30 in | Bust ~34–36 in. Sister sizes: **32D / 32DD**.
- **30E / 30F / 30G Bra Canada:** Underbust ~29–30 in | Bust ~35–37 in. Full-cup small-ribcage profile.

### 3. Band Size 32 Profiles
- **32AA Bra Canada:** Underbust ~31–32 in | Bust ~31–32 in. Extremely shallow cup profile.
- **32D Bra Canada / 32DD Bra Canada:** Underbust ~31–32 in | Bust ~35–37 in. Sister size: **34C / 34D**.
- **32DDD Bra Canada / 32E / 32F / 32FF / 32G / 32H Bra Canada:** Underbust ~31–32 in | Bust ~37–40 in. Full bust profiles requiring strong side support wings.

## Standard Medium Band Profiles (Bands 34, 36)

Bands 34 and 36 represent the most widely manufactured bra band sizes in North America.

### 1. Band Size 34 Profiles
- **34AA Bra Canada:** Underbust ~33–34 in | Bust ~33–34 in. Shallow chest profile.
- **34DD Bra Canada:** Underbust ~33–34 in | Bust ~38–39 in. Sister size: **36D**. One of Canada's top 5 measured sizes.
- **34DDD Bras Canada (34 DDD / 34E):** Underbust ~33–34 in | Bust ~39–40 in. Sister size: **36DD**. Deep contour or multi-part seamed cups recommended.
- **34F / 34G / 34H / 34J Bra Canada:** Underbust ~33–34 in | Bust ~40–44 in. Fuller bust profile requiring high-tensile flex wire support.

### 2. Band Size 36 Profiles
- **36A Bra Canada / 36AA Bra Canada:** Underbust ~35–36 in | Bust ~36–37 in. Shallow profile for average ribcage.
- **36DD Bras Canada:** Underbust ~35–36 in | Bust ~40–41 in. Sister size: **38D**.
- **36DDD Bra Canada (36DDD):** Underbust ~35–36 in | Bust ~41–42 in. Sister size: **38DD**.
- **36F / 36G / 36H / 36I / 36J Bra Canada:** Underbust ~35–36 in | Bust ~42–46 in. Heavy bust profile requiring wide leotard back wings.
