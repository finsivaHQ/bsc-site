## Deep Dive: Sizing Audit by Brand

### 1. Jockey Bra Size Calculator India & Fit Dynamics
Jockey is one of the most popular daily-wear underwear brands in India. A frequent question is whether a **Jockey bra size calculator in inches** differs from international standards.
* **Band Elastic:** Jockey uses high-quality combed cotton blended with elastane. The bands offer firm control, meaning if your underbust measures 31 inches, round up to a 32 band rather than squeezing into a 30 band.
* **Cup Shape:** Jockey cups are generally designed for average root width and shallow-to-medium projection.

### 2. Enamor Bra Size Calculator India
Enamor specializes in structured, molded T-shirt bras and festive lingerie.
* Because Enamor molded cups keep a rigid shape, breast tissue that is fuller at the top may experience slight cup spillage (quad-boob). If you are between cup sizes (e.g., C and D), choose the larger cup size in Enamor.

### 3. Shyaway & Triumph Bra Size Calculator India
* **Shyaway Bra Size Calculator India:** Follows standard Indian sizing closely across everyday, bridal, and lace bralette collections.
* **Triumph Bra Size Calculator India:** Premium European construction that labels both US/UK inch bands (34, 36) and EU centimeter bands (75, 80). Refer to our [Bra Size Chart](/bra-size-chart) for exact EU-to-IN cross-references.

### 4. Marks & Spencer (M&S) India Bra Sizing
M&S stores across India adhere strictly to British (UK) cup progressions. If you wear an Indian DD cup, M&S DD will fit identically, but if you need a 6th cup step, select M&S E cup (which equals US DDD/F). ## 3 Fitting Checks for Your New Bra

1. **The 2-Finger Band Test:** You should be able to slide no more than two fingers under the back band when fastened on the loosest hook.
2. **Gore Laying Flat:** The center bridge between the cups (the gore) must rest flat against your sternum without floating.
3. **Smooth Cup Transition:** The top edge of the cup should lay smooth against your chest without cutting into breast tissue or showing gaping under T-shirts.
