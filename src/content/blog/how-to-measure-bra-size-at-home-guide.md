Asking yourself *"What bra size am I?"* or *"Where can I check my bra size?"* is one of the most common questions women have. Studies show that up to **80% of women wear the wrong bra size**, often enduring tight shoulder straps, digging underwires, or gaping cups without realizing their size has changed.

Measuring your bra size at home is easy, private, and requires only a few minutes. In this master guide, we explain **where to measure bra size**, **how to measure bra cup size**, and how to convert those numbers into your perfect fit.

## What You Need to Measure Bra Size at Home

Before starting, gather the following tools:
1. **A Soft Cloth Measuring Tape**: Standard flexible tailor's tape calibrated in inches or centimeters.
2. **Alternative (No Measuring Tape)**: A length of non-stretch string, ribbon, or cord, plus a ruler.
3. **A Mirror**: Helps ensure the tape remains level across your back.
4. **An Unpadded Bra**: Wear a thin, unpadded bra that lifts without altering your natural shape.

---

## Step 1: Where & How to Measure Band Size (Underbust)

Your bra band provides **80% of the support**. If the band is too loose, your shoulder straps will dig in and cause fatigue.

```
Step 1: Underbust (Band) Measurement
   |
   v
[ Measure snugly around ribcage, right under breasts. Keep tape level! ]
```

1. **Position the Tape**: Wrap the measuring tape directly around your ribcage, immediately beneath your bust fold.
2. **Check the Fit**: The tape should be snug against your skin—snugger than you want the final bra band to feel, as bra elastics stretch.
3. **Keep It Level**: Look in the mirror to verify the tape is straight across your back, parallel to the floor.
4. **Record the Number**: Note the measurement in inches (e.g., 31.5 inches). 
5. **Round the Band**: If your measurement is an even number, that is your baseline band size. If it is an odd number or fraction, standard algorithms round up or test adjacent sizes depending on band stretch.

---

## Step 2: Where & How to Measure Cup Size (Overbust)

Cup size measures breast projection relative to your ribcage structure.

```
Step 2: Overbust (Cup) Measurement
   |
   v
[ Wrap tape loosely around fullest part of bust. Do not press tissue! ]
```

1. **Position the Tape**: Wrap the measuring tape around the fullest part of your bust (usually level with your nipples).
2. **Keep It Loose & Gentle**: Do **not** pull the tape tight across your breasts. It should rest gently against your tissue without indenting.
3. **Breathe Naturally**: Take a relaxed breath out before noting the number.
4. **Record the Number**: Note the measurement in inches (e.g., 36 inches).

---

## Step 3: Calculating Your Cup Letter

To find your cup letter, subtract your Underbust Band measurement from your Overbust Bust measurement:

$$\text{Difference (Inches)} = \text{Overbust (Bust)} - \text{Underbust (Band)}$$

Match your difference to the standard cup chart:

```
Difference (Inches) | US Cup Size | UK Cup Size | Description
--------------------|-------------|-------------|----------------------------
0 - 0.5 inches      | AA          | AA          | Very slight projection
1 inch              | A           | A           | 1" bust-to-band difference
2 inches            | B           | B           | 2" bust-to-band difference
3 inches            | C           | C           | 3" bust-to-band difference
4 inches            | D           | D           | 4" bust-to-band difference
5 inches            | DD          | DD          | 5" bust-to-band difference
6 inches            | DDD / F     | E           | 6" bust-to-band difference
7 inches            | G           | F           | 7" bust-to-band difference
8 inches            | H           | FF          | 8" bust-to-band difference
```

### Example Calculation:
- **Underbust (Band)** = 32 inches
- **Overbust (Bust)** = 36 inches
- **Difference** = $36 - 32 = 4\text{ inches}$
- **Calculated Bra Size** = **32D**

## How to Measure Without a Measuring Tape at Home

If you don't own a soft tape measure, use the **String & Ruler Method**:

1. Take a non-stretchy string, shoelace, or phone charging cable.
2. Wrap it around your ribcage (underbust) as described in Step 1, and mark the overlap point with a pen.
3. Lay the string flat against a desk ruler or hardware measuring tape to read the inches.
4. Repeat the process for your fullest bust measurement (overbust).
5. Enter both numbers into our [Bra Size Calculator](/#calculator).

## Common Measuring Pitfalls & How to Avoid Them

- ❌ **Measuring over a thick padded bra**: Adds artificial inches to your cup size.
- ❌ **Slouching or holding breath**: Causes underbust measurements to be off by up to 2 inches.
- ❌ **Tape drooping down your back**: Makes your underbust reading falsely large, leading to a loose band.

## Professional Expert Advice

> [!NOTE]
> **Fit Expert Recommendation**:
> Tape measurements provide your **starting baseline size**. Breast shape (shallow, projected, bell-shaped, side-set) heavily influences how a bra fits in real life. Always check that the center gore (bridge) of the bra lays flat against your sternum and that no breast tissue spills over the cup tops or sides.

For further anatomical guidance on breast health and proper garment support, consult resources from the [NHS Bra Fitting Guidelines](https://www.nhs.uk/).
