<details class="bg-surface border border-hairline rounded-xl p-5 mt-4 cursor-pointer">
  <summary class="font-bold text-ink list-none flex justify-between">
    <span>Why does the calculator ask for my leaning and lying down measurements?</span>
  </summary>
  <p class="mt-4 text-body text-sm">For large and pendulous breasts, gravity pulls breast tissue down when standing, which can mask the true volume. Leaning forward allows all the tissue to hang free, providing the most accurate representation of the breast tissue that needs to be encapsulated by the bra cups.</p>
</details>

<details class="bg-surface border border-hairline rounded-xl p-5 mt-4 cursor-pointer">
  <summary class="font-bold text-ink list-none flex justify-between">
    <span>Why are seamless "T-shirt" bras so hard to fit for a large bust?</span>
  </summary>
  <p class="mt-4 text-body text-sm">Molded T-shirt bras are created by stretching a single piece of foam over a hot metal mold. This process can only create a wide, shallow shape. Large breasts are typically highly projected (they stick out more than they are wide). Trying to put a projected breast into a shallow molded cup is like trying to fit an orange into a bowl—it doesn't work well and causes gaps or wire digging.</p>
</details>

<details class="bg-surface border border-hairline rounded-xl p-5 mt-4 cursor-pointer">
  <summary class="font-bold text-ink list-none flex justify-between">
    <span>I have back fat and "rolls" when I wear a bra. Does this mean the band is too tight?</span>
  </summary>
  <p class="mt-4 text-body text-sm">Not necessarily! A properly supportive band has to grip your body firmly to support a heavy bust. This grip will naturally displace soft tissue (flesh/fat), creating a slight bulge. This is totally normal and means the bra is doing its job. If the band is comfortable and parallel to the floor, do not size up just to eliminate the "squish"—you will lose all your support.</p>
</details>

<details class="bg-surface border border-hairline rounded-xl p-5 mt-4 cursor-pointer">
