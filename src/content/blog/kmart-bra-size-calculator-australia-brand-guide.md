## Key Takeaways

* **34C Equals AU 12C:** A US/UK 34C translates directly to **Australian Size 12C (AU 12C)** in retail stores like Kmart, Target AU, and Myer.
* **Kmart Anko Sizing Standard:** Kmart Australia follows standard Australian dress size bands (AU 8 to AU 22) with A, B, C, D, DD, and E cup options.
* **Beginner Measuring Rule:** Measure direct underbust without adding 4 inches to ensure reliable online shopping fits across Kmart, M&S, and EU brands.

Shopping for everyday underwear in Australia—whether at budget retailers like Kmart Australia (Anko brand), department stores like Myer and David Jones, or ordering from international stores like Marks & Spencer—requires navigating different band label formats.

## Trade-Offs: Australian vs US vs EU Retail Brands

When buying bras in Australia from different retail sources:

1. **Kmart Australia (Anko):** Soft fabrics with generous band stretch. If you are between band sizes (e.g., between AU 10 and AU 12), start with the smaller band (AU 10) as soft bands relax quickly with wear.
2. **Marks & Spencer (M&S Australia):** Uses British band numbers (32, 34, 36) and UK cup steps (DD, E, F, FF). Learn more in our [M&S Bra Size Guide](/blog/marks-and-spencer-bra-size-calculator-differences).
3. **European Brands (Triumph AU, Hunkemöller):** Label bands in centimeters (70, 75, 80). Use a **bra size calculator EU** mode to translate centimeters to AU dress sizes effortlessly.

## 3 Online Shopping Consistency Tips

* **Always Check Hook Placement:** A new bra should fit snugly on the **outermost (loosest) hook** so you can tighten it as the band stretches over time.
* **Verify Cup Gore Fit:** The center front gore of the bra should sit flat against your sternum. If it tilts forward, step up one cup size.
* **Sister Size Backup:** If your calculated size (e.g., AU 12C) feels tight in the band, try sister size **AU 14B** for a looser band with identical cup volume. Learn more in our [Sister Sizes Explained](/blog/sister-sizes-explained) guide.
