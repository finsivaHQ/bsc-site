## Step-by-Step Instructions: Measuring at Home

To get an accurate measurement at home, you need a flexible fabric measuring tape and a mirror. Wear an unpadded, non-push-up bra while measuring.

```
Step 1: Measure Underbust (Ribcage Band)
└─> Wrap tape snugly directly beneath your breasts.
    Keep tape level across your back. Read in inches or cm.

Step 2: Measure Overbust (Full Bust Volume)
└─> Wrap tape loosely around the fullest part of your bust (across nipples).
    Do not pull tight or compress breast tissue.

Step 3: Subtract & Determine Cup Size
└─> (Overbust - Underbust) = Cup Difference
    1" = A | 2" = B | 3" = C | 4" = D | 5" = DD
```

## 1. Measuring in Inches (Indian / US / UK Standard)

If you are using a measuring tape marked in inches for a **bra size calculator India in inches**:

1. **Calculate Band Size:** 
   - Measure your ribcage snugly.
   - If your underbust measurement is an even number (e.g., 32 inches), your baseline band size is **32**.
   - If your underbust measurement is an odd number (e.g., 31 inches or 33 inches), round up to the nearest even number (31" becomes **32 band**, 33" becomes **34 band**).
2. **Calculate Cup Size:**
   - Subtract your rounded band size from your full overbust measurement.
   - Example: Underbust = 31" (rounded to 32 band). Overbust = 35". Difference = 35" - 32" = 3 inches (C Cup).
   - Recommended Size = **32C**.

---

## 2. Measuring in Centimeters (cm) (EU / International Standard)

If your tape measure is in centimeters or you are shopping for European brands requiring a **bra size calculator cm India**:

1. **Calculate EU Band Size:**
   - Measure your underbust in centimeters (e.g., 74 cm).
   - Round to the nearest multiple of 5 (74 cm rounds to **75 cm EU band**).
2. **Calculate EU Cup Size:**
   - Subtract underbust (cm) from overbust (cm).
   - Example: Underbust = 74 cm, Overbust = 89 cm. Difference = 15 cm.
   - A 15 cm difference corresponds to a **B / C cup in EU sizing** (75B or 75C).

## The Outdated "+4 Method" Explained

Many legacy bra charts in India advise adding 4 inches to your underbust measurement (e.g., if underbust is 28", adding 4 gives a 32 band).
